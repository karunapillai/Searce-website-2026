AI Adoption & Change Engineering

Make the change land in people, and prove it.

Adoption is where transformations quietly die. The question is no longer which tools people get. It is which work people still do, and whether anything actually changed.

See the offerings
Deployed Adopted

The gap between what was deployed and what was actually adopted. Closing it is the reason this practice exists.

AI is not another tool in the stack. It is a change in what work is.

Every workplace platform before this one asked people to learn a new interface and keep doing the same job. This one moves the job.

Tasks that defined a role for a decade become something a system handles in the background. What remains of that role is a different job — and usually a more human one. That is a work design problem before it is a technology problem.

The question we used to ask The question that matters now

Which tools do people get?

→Which work do people still do?

Train people on the software.

→Redesign the role around the capability.

Count licences and logins.

→Measure the work that actually changed.

Roll it out to everyone.

→Build it with the people it changes.

Productivity is the same work, faster.

→Productivity is work that was not possible before.
