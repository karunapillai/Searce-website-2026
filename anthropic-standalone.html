Which Claude for which work?

Each Claude model has a job it does best. Sort the lineup, map capability against price, compare two models side by side, or start from your workload. Specifications come from Anthropic; capability positions are Searce’s indicative assessment.

intelligent model routing

Every request, routed on its merits.

A router looks at each request (how complex it is, how fast the answer is needed, how much it has to read and what it costs) and sends it to the model that fits. The same fast model can answer a customer on its own or work as a sub-agent inside a long investigation. Pick a request to see its route.

Lower cost per outcome

Most requests don’t need the most capable model. Sending them to faster, lower-priced models keeps spend tied to the real difficulty of the work.

Speed where people are waiting

Customer-facing and real-time steps go to the lowest-latency models, so answers arrive in the flow of work.

Depth and review where it counts

High-stakes and long-running work goes to the most capable models, with people approving the decisions that matter.

for your systemsopen standard

Model Context Protocol (MCP)

An open standard for connecting AI models to tools and data sources, introduced by Anthropic and now governed by the Agentic AI Foundation.

Where it fitsOne consistent way to give Claude access to your systems of record.

How we helpWe build and secure MCP servers for your core systems, with permissions that match your policies.

for your systemsopen standard

Agent Skills

An open standard for packaging instructions, scripts and resources that agents load when a task calls for them. First published by Anthropic and donated to the Agentic AI Foundation.

Where it fitsEncoding your procedures once so Claude and other compatible agents follow them consistently.

How we helpWe turn playbooks and procedures into tested, versioned skills.
