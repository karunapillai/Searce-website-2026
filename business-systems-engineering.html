The buy-versus-build line has moved. Workflow tooling that once required eighteen months and a licence can now be built in weeks, and the enterprises acting on that are rebuilding the software layer around how their business actually runs rather than configuring someone else's product around it.

AI Outcome Engineering does both halves of that work in one squad: re-engineering the process and building the system that runs it.

Product engineers, software and AI engineers, business process engineers and industrial engineers work together, because process redesign without build authority produces a recommendation, and building without process authority produces a faster version of work that should not exist.

Systems of record stay where they are. Searce works the layer above them: the workflows, the internal tooling, the orchestration, and the custom systems that carry competitive advantage.
