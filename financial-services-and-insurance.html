The operating reality

Decisions made under time pressure against documents that are unstructured, incomplete, and scattered.

Every core workflow is a high-stakes search-and-judgment task against documents scattered across legacy mainframes, policy engines, and modern SaaS. And regulators add an uncompromising constraint: every automated output must be audit-ready and fully explainable, which is why black-box solutions stall at the risk committee.

The processes this industry runs on

Ten workflows where speed, accuracy, and auditability all have to hold at once.

Underwriting & Risk Assessment
Submission intake, unstructured data extraction, risk model execution, decisioning, and exception referrals.
Claims Handling & Settlement
FNOL, triage routing, fraud investigation, reserve setting, settlement, and subrogation recovery.
Policy Servicing & Adjustments
Endorsements, cancellations, renewals, policyholder queries, and coverage verification.
Customer Onboarding & KYC/AML
Identity verification, sanction screening, beneficial ownership documentation, and approval routing.
Credit Decisioning & Loan Origination
Application ingestion, income/asset verification, credit scoring, exception handling, and closing.
Collections & Debt Recovery
Borrower segmentation, automated contact strategy, repayment arrangement, and escalation paths.
Financial Crime & AML
Alert triage, transaction monitoring investigation, case file compilation, and SAR filing.
Document Search & Retrieval
Policy wordings, reinsurance treaties, correspondence history, and prior legal precedents.
Regulatory Reporting & Audit
Data assembly, cross-system reconciliation, submission filing, and audit trail generation.
Complaints & Dispute Resolution
Intake classification, root cause analysis, redress calculation, and regulatory reporting.
