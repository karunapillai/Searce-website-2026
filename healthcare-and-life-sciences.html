The operating reality

Care and discovery are constrained by how fast humans extract insights from unstructured medical text.

Clinicians spend up to 40% of their shifts navigating EHRs and transcribing visits. Research teams wait months to find trial-eligible candidates in unstructured records, and revenue cycle teams fight high denial rates from manual coding errors, all under HIPAA, FDA, and GDPR.

Clinical Trial Recruitment & Enrollment

Prior Authorization & Insurance Verification
