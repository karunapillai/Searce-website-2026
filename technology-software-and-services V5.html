Software is being rewritten.

The SaaS playbook was built for people using software. The next one is being built for software doing the work.

  1. AI is compressing development cycles, changing what customers expect, and making features easier to replicate.

  2. The question is no longer where to add AI. It is what remains defensible when intelligence becomes abundant.

  3. The next generation of software companies will rethink the product, the engineering system, and the business model around autonomous execution and measurable outcomes.

The new software equation

AI is changing where software creates value, how that value is delivered, and how technology companies compete.

As agents perform more of the work, value is increasingly measured by what the software accomplishes, not simply how many people access it.

Products are evolving beyond interfaces that help users complete tasks into systems that can reason, act, and execute work.

As individual AI capabilities become easier to replicate, differentiation increasingly comes from proprietary context, data, and ownership of critical workflows.

AI can accelerate coding, but real speed depends on the entire engineering system, from architecture and testing to security, governance, and deployment.

As agents operate across applications, models, APIs, and data, the ability to coordinate these systems securely and reliably becomes a core product capability.

Baseline

Legacy on-premise Cloudera/Hive clusters restricted data access speeds for conversational AI models, while 100 TB of volatile tables caused severe ingestion bottlenecks.

Intervention

Migrated a 60 TB Hive database into BigQuery in under 5 days using distcp scripts, moved 40 TB of HBase tables into GCS via multi-threaded Python pipelines, and deployed Terraform Landing Zones.

Result

Slashed platform maintenance overhead by 95%, compressed pipeline ingestion windows from 10 minutes to under 3.5 minutes, and doubled baseline data and compute processing capacity.
