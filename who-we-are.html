Twenty-twoyears of solvingfor better.

searce [ˈsɪrs] · noun · a fine sieve.

To refine, to analyze, to improve. The name is the commitment: question the status quo, iterate relentlessly, leave things better than we found them.

Searce was founded in 2004 in process consulting: understanding how work actually gets done and making it better. That DNA never left. We added cloud and data engineering depth as the platforms matured, applied AI as the models arrived, and today we engineer AI outcomes for the agentic enterprise.

Most firms in this market were born last year. We have crossed multiple technology discontinuities and come out stronger each time. When the current hype cycle ends, we will still be here, and so will the outcomes.

Project Better Living

Led by Bala Kishore, Project Better Living is designed to continue to remind us that our foremost objective is to become a better version of ourselves every day. As a cornerstone of this initiative, we share our happier culture and practical tools and methods that Searcians, clients, and partners can readily adopt. Through workshops, knowledge-sharing platforms, and family events, Project Better Living empowers everyone to embrace continuous improvement and build a fulfilling personal and professional life.
